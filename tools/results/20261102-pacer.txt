- Without the pacer the result depends on the network minute: 94.4-99.5 %
  single-refresh pictures, repeats in 13-24 of 29 seconds. With it 99.8-99.9 %
  in every pair, never a picture held longer than two refreshes.
- "phase ms" (ml-flips.py, where in the refresh KWin's flip lands relative to
  the player's swap) jumps between ~2.5 and ~5.8 ms without the pacer and the
  repeats cluster in those seconds: pictures arrive bunched, the newest-wins
  path shows two in one refresh and none in the next. With the pacer the phase
  is flat (~2.4 ms) in pairs 1 and 3.
- Cost: decoder->swap +10-14 ms p50. Part of it is not new latency on screen:
  without the pacer the swap returns at once but KWin still holds the buffer
  until its next latch, so the difference that reaches the eye is about one
  refresh (8.3 ms) plus the 0-2 pictures the queue holds after a burst.
- Pacer rule (Moonlight's): queue up to 3 decoded pictures, show the oldest
  each refresh; if the queue stayed above 1 for half a second, drop one to
  catch up. The "跳过" counts (5-20 per minute) are those catch-ups and
  decoder overruns, same order with and without the pacer.

Decision: pacer on by default ("流畅"), "低延迟" one tap away in the settings
page (saved as pace=0 in app.ini; YY_PACE overrides it for a run).
