## Verdict
Direct stateful V4L2 + VIDIOC_EXPBUF is 1.1x (AV1) to 2.7x (HEVC 2560x1600) lower latency than VA-API
(qcom-va-driver spends ~4.6 ms inside send_packet for H.264/HEVC alone). The player decodes through V4L2 directly.
